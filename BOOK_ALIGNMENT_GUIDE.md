# Quantum City — 30-program research alignment

Based on the author's *Quantum City: A Practical Guide to Smart-City Strategy, Pilots, Governance and Measurable* (2026).

## Central principle
Begin with a measurable urban problem. Assess readiness, compare against a strong classical baseline, pilot with controls, measure outcomes and govern scale-up. No model alone proves quantum advantage or a successful city deployment.

## Standard evidence gate
1. Named city problem and accountable agency
2. Data availability, quality and permissions
3. Conventional technology baseline
4. Quantum relevance or enabling technology rationale
5. Pilot hypothesis and reproducible protocol
6. KPI target, measurement period and independent evaluation
7. Cybersecurity, privacy, procurement and lifecycle risks
8. Cost, benefit and environmental accounting
9. Explicit proceed / stop / scale decision
10. Auditable evidence exports

## Boundaries
Carbon footprint models do not become quantum applications merely because they are on this dashboard. Carbon nanomaterial manufacturing is not inherently quantum computing. NV diamond sensing and QKD modules represent simplified physics or link-budget models, not field performance. Hardware results from IBM are historical user-supplied observations, not fresh live experiments.

The Q-CITY steps are: DEFINE → ASSESS → SELECT → PRIORITIZE → PILOT → BENCHMARK → INTEGRATE → MEASURE → SCALE → GOVERN.
